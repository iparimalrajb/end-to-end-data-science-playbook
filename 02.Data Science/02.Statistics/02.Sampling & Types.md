# What is Sampling ?

    Sampling means selecting a small subset of data (sample) from a large dataset (population) to analyze and draw conclusions.

    ( Sampling = Taking a small part to understand the whole )

    Example :
        Imagine cooking rice:
	•	You don’t check every grain 🍚
	•	You take a spoonful (sample)
	•	If it’s cooked → assume all rice is cooked

        👉 That spoon = sample
        👉 Whole pot = population
